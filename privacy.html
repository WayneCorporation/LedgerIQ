Privacy Notice

Effective: 15 August 2026

Roles

ledgerIQ operator is the responsible party for account, billing, support and service-usage information. Enterprise customers are normally the responsible party for people and transaction data they enter; ledgerIQ acts as their operator under a data-processing agreement.

Information we process

We process account and team details, roles, authentication data, audit events, subscription records, client contacts, invoices, expenses, uploaded documents, bank-feed transaction data, accounting and payroll summaries, API activity, support messages and security logs. We do not store payment card numbers.

Purposes

We process information to perform the service contract, run customer-selected integrations, secure accounts, send business documents, reconcile records, provide support, meet legal obligations and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information or use customer financial data for advertising.

Providers and cross-border transfers

Hosting, storage, email, monitoring, banking, accounting, payroll and payment providers may process limited information under written terms. Before launch, list the actual providers, countries, purposes and POPIA section 72 safeguards in the subprocessor schedule.

Retention

Workspace information is retained while an account is active and for the contractually stated period afterward. Backups expire on a documented rolling schedule. Some accounting, tax, fraud-prevention and dispute records may require longer retention.

Security and incidents

Controls include tenant and company separation, role-based access, encrypted transport, encrypted integration secrets, hashed passwords and sessions, signed webhooks, audit history and backups. If a qualifying compromise occurs, we will notify the Information Regulator and affected people as required by POPIA.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion; object to certain processing; withdraw consent where it applies; and complain to South Africa's Information Regulator. Contact the Information Officer at privacy@ledgeriq.app.

Cookies

The app uses a strictly necessary authentication cookie. Marketing analytics must not be enabled until this notice and any required consent controls are updated.
