# Enterprise customer onboarding runbook

## Discovery and contract

- Name executive sponsor, project owner, finance owner, security/privacy contact and technical contact.
- Confirm companies, users, roles, approval thresholds, currencies, tax status, integrations, migration scope and success measures.
- Sign MSA, order form, DPA and SLA; collect implementation fee before starting.

## Configure and migrate

- Create the customer organisations and owner/admin accounts.
- Approve the user list and least-privilege roles; remove test access.
- Export, clean, map and reconcile clients, open invoices, expenses and opening balances.
- Connect providers in sandbox, document consent, then promote approved credentials to production.
- Configure invoice identity, tax/VAT details, payment details, approval flow, API keys and webhook allowlist.

## Validate and launch

- Customer signs off record counts, totals, samples and reconciliation.
- Run role/access, approval, integration failure, webhook retry, backup restore and incident-contact tests.
- Train admins, finance users and approvers; provide recordings and quick-reference material.
- Agree cutover time, freeze window, rollback owner and hypercare hours.
- Obtain written go-live acceptance and record all exceptions.

## First 30 days

- Check sync failures and unmatched transactions daily during week one.
- Hold weekly adoption/reconciliation reviews during the first month.
- Close migration access, rotate temporary credentials and archive signed acceptance.
- Schedule quarterly business review and access review.
